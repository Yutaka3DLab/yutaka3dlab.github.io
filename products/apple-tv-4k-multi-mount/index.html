Installation flexibility

Four ways to mount it.

Use the installation method that works best for your TV and room. The same holder supports all four options.

01

VESA mount

Attach the holder to a compatible VESA mounting point on the back of your TV using suitable VESA hardware.

VESA screws are not included.
02

Wall mount

Secure the empty holder to drywall using the included screws and anchors, then slide the Apple TV into place.

03

TV vent hooks

Insert the included hooks into compatible ventilation slots and hang the holder behind the display.

TV vent shape and spacing vary by model; confirm a secure fit before use.
04

3M adhesive

Mount to a clean, flat compatible surface using the included 3M Command strips.

Follow the adhesive manufacturer's surface-preparation, pressure, and cure instructions.
