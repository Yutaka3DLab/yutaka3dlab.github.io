About Yutaka3DLab

Small studio. Practical ideas. Real-world testing.

Yutaka3DLab is an independent design studio focused on functional 3D printed products for smart homes, media setups, cable management, and everyday organization.

Products are designed from scratch, prototyped through multiple iterations, and tested with real hardware before release. The goal is simple: create clean, useful solutions that feel considered from installation through daily use.
